  • Performance of a contract or pre-contractual steps (Art. 6(1)(b)): to process the quiz and to sell and deliver the digital guides.

  • Our legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): to keep the Site secure, prevent abuse and produce aggregate statistics.
  • Legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c)): to meet tax and accounting requirements related to the sale of digital content.

8Your rights

You can exercise the rights provided by Articles 15–22 of the GDPR at any time, including the right to:

  • Access: confirm whether we process your personal information and get a copy of it.
  • Correction: have inaccurate information corrected or incomplete information completed.
  • Deletion ("right to be forgotten"): have your information deleted, where the law allows.
  • Restriction: limit how we process your information in certain circumstances.
  • Portability: receive the information you gave us in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Objection: object at any time to processing based on our legitimate interests.
  • Withdrawal of consent: withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting processing that happened before.
  • Complaint: file a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (www.garanteprivacy.it) or with the supervisory authority where you live, if you believe we are breaking the law.

To exercise any of these rights, just email us at the address in Section 14.
