FAQ

Before you start.

Is the quiz really free and easy to use?

Yes. Seven short questions, no email, no sign-up. Answer them and get your skin profile right away, free forever. Only the recommended guide is paid.

Can I buy a guide without taking the quiz?

Yes, browse the library and pick the guide you want. The quiz just helps you avoid buying the wrong one and wasting money on the wrong products.

Does the AI scan work with old photos?

It works best with front-facing photos in natural light, without filters. Blurry or dark photos make the zone-by-zone analysis less accurate.

Do I need a lot of products?

No. The routines use a handful of products, morning and evening, in the right order, and every guide includes a budget section so you know what to spend.

What if I need another guide later?

You can buy more guides at any time at the same single price. Or get the full bundle and save 49% on all six.

Does the AI analysis replace a dermatologist?

No. The AI scan is a starting point. For serious concerns like severe acne, rosacea or eczema, always see a dermatologist.
